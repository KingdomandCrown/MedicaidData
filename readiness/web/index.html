What this is not

This is a developmental instrument. It is not a performance review, not a selection tool, and not a record for employment decisions. Your results may not be used for hiring, promotion, compensation, or termination.

Your individual results are yours. A manager sees only aggregates, and only once enough people have completed it that no one is identifiable. Individual results reach a manager only if you explicitly release them, and you can revoke that.

Answer honestly rather than aspirationally — the plan you get is only as useful as the picture you give it. Do not enter patient, member, or otherwise protected information anywhere in this assessment.
